Tips & Tricks

Ten small things that make every trip better

Hard-won lessons from the road — short, practical and (mostly) learned the hard way.

01

Communicate & Respect

Greet people in their language, ask before you photograph, and listen more than you talk.

02

Don’t forget your charger

Phone, camera, power bank — and the right plug adapter. Nothing ends a photo day faster than 0%.

03

Be Thankful

A sincere thank-you opens more doors than any guidebook. Say it often, and mean it.

04

Try to find out what is ‘normal’?

Every place has its own rhythm: meal times, dress codes, tipping. Observe first, then join in.

05

Pack the right things

Pack light, pack layers, and leave room for what you will bring back.

06

Don’t forget your socks

The small things make the difference — dry socks win long days on foot.

07

A/C can kill you on your trip!

Buses, trains and hotels are often freezing. Keep a light jacket within reach.

08

Knowledge – acquire it

Read, ask, visit the museum nobody mentioned. Curiosity is the best travel insurance.

09

Smile – keep it

It is the one phrase that works in every language.

10

Love – value it

People, places, moments — the things you love are what you will remember.
