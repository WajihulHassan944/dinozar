A little knowledge base

Terms, places and ideas that keep coming up in the stories — explained in a few lines.

AAEHTEducation
The Association of European Hotel and Tourism Schools — a network of schools that also runs European championships where students compete in hospitality and tourism disciplines.
CCOP24Climate
The 24th UN climate conference of the parties, held in Katowice, Poland, in December 2018. It produced the rulebook for putting the Paris Agreement into practice.
DDesign thinkingMethod
A human-centred, iterative way of solving problems: empathise with people, define the problem, ideate, prototype and test — then repeat.
KKaospilotEducation
A school for creative leadership and social entrepreneurship in Aarhus, Denmark, mixing business, design and project-based learning.
LLateral thinkingMethod
Solving problems through indirect, creative approaches instead of step-by-step logic — a term popularised by Edward de Bono.
OÖ3Media
Austria’s popular public radio station. A call-out on Ö3 for dream summer jobs led to the 2016 Olympic internship in Rio.
RRio 2016Travel
The Games of the XXXI Olympiad, held in Rio de Janeiro in August 2016. The travel diary covers the weeks around them.
SSDGsSustainability
The UN Sustainable Development Goals. SDG 9 — industry, innovation and infrastructure — features in several stories.
SSlow travelSustainability
Choosing slower, lower-emission ways of getting there — like the train to Katowice — and treating the journey as part of the trip.
